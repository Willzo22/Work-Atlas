## 2️⃣ Validate before you build

**Rule:** no product gets built further until it clears a pre-sell test. 7 days per product, max three at a time.

1. **Day 1:** One-page landing page or PayFast payment link per product: name, 3 bullets, price, "ships by [date]". Preview images are screenshots from what already exists.
2. **Day 2 to 5:** Show it where the buyer already is. One IG/Threads post, one WhatsApp broadcast, 10 DMs to people who fit (freelancers you know, small-business owners, the Joburg lead list contacts).
3. **Day 7:** Count. **Pass = 3 paid pre-orders or 10 specific "yes, send it" replies.** Likes don't count.
4. **Fail:** change the price or the buyer once, re-test once, then drop it.
